# Agentic Consultancy Economy (UK) — Investor Research Report
Date: 3 March 2026  
Prepared for: Agent Ops investment narrative and deck development

## 1) Executive Summary

- The UK has crossed from AI experimentation to active deployment: **52%** of UK businesses with 10+ employees report using at least one AI technology, and **18%** already use generative AI. Adoption is materially higher in larger firms.
- Financial services, one of the highest-value UK verticals, is already in execution mode: **75%** of surveyed firms use AI, with another **10%** planning adoption within three years.
- The broader UK consulting market remains large and growing (**£23bn in 2024**, **+7.3% YoY**), creating room for specialist agentic implementation practices.
- Enterprise demand is real, but execution failure risk is high: Gartner projects **>40%** of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by end-2027 due to cost, risk, or unclear value. This directly supports a productized “implementation + governance” consultancy model.
- Monetization evidence is strong across three layers:
  - Contractor/freelancer rates: median UK day rates around **£638–£750/day** for AI-related roles.
  - Productized agency services: live market offers from **~£850/month retainers** up to **~£9,997/month**.
  - Enterprise consulting scale: Accenture reported **$5.6bn** in FY2025 generative AI bookings.
- A hybrid model (software license + installation + retainer) is viable and aligns with proven Laravel commercial packaging patterns (Nova/Spark).

## 2) Market Context: UK Demand for Agentic Workflow Consulting

### 2.1 Adoption and readiness

- UK businesses (10+ employees):
  - **52%** used at least one AI technology in Q4 2024.
  - **18%** used generative AI.
  - Large businesses (250+ employees): **68%** used at least one AI technology; **33%** used generative AI.
- UK financial services:
  - **75%** of firms already using AI.
  - Additional **10%** planning adoption in next 3 years.

### 2.2 Barriers that create consultancy demand

- Top barriers among UK businesses not yet using AI:
  - Data security risk concerns (**40%**).
  - Cost concerns (**37%**).
  - Insufficient skills/training (**32%**).
  - Accuracy/bias concerns (**30%**).
- Interpretation: these are implementation-and-governance barriers, not “awareness” barriers, which supports paid hands-on consultancy.

### 2.3 Macro consulting and AI growth

- UK consulting industry:
  - **£23bn** market in 2024, **7.3% annual growth**.
- UK AI ecosystem momentum:
  - UK AI companies raised **£2.9bn** in private investment in 2024.
  - Average UK AI startup deal size reported at **£5.9m** (highest in Europe in the cited government release).

## 3) Agentic Economy Signal: Why “Build + Operate” Consulting Wins

- Gartner forecasts for 2028:
  - **33%** of enterprise software applications expected to include agentic AI (up from <1% in 2024).
  - **15%** of day-to-day work decisions expected to be autonomous (up from 0% in 2024).
  - **>40%** of agentic AI projects likely cancelled by end-2027 without stronger controls and value framing.
- Investor implication:
  - The opportunity is not only “building agents”.
  - The monetizable wedge is **reliable deployment**: scoping, guardrails, integration, observability, ROI reporting, and iterative optimization.

## 4) Pricing and Earnings Benchmarks (What People Are Making)

## 4.1 UK daily rates (contract market)

- AI Consultant median UK day rate: **£638/day**.
- Machine Learning median UK day rate: **£675/day**.
- Prompt Engineering median UK day rate: **£750/day**.
- Public-sector marketplace examples for AI consulting services show approximately **£500/day to £1,500/day**.

## 4.2 Productized agency offers (market-visible)

- Example A (GBP pricing): **£1,497/mo**, **£4,997/mo**, **£9,997/mo** tiers.
- Example B: one-off setup (**£2,000–£5,000**) plus recurring retainer (**£850–£1,500/mo**).

## 4.3 Enterprise-level signal

- Accenture reported:
  - **$1.5bn** Q4 FY2025 new generative AI bookings.
  - **$5.6bn** FY2025 generative AI bookings.
  - **$2.1bn** FY2025 generative AI revenue.
- Interpretation: budget pools exist at enterprise scale; mid-market consultancies can capture earlier-stage implementation and managed operations.

## 4.4 Practical revenue math (for your model)

Assumption set grounded in observed day-rates + market retainers:

- Solo specialist model:
  - 130 billable days/year × £650/day = **£84.5k services revenue**.
  - Plus 4 retainers × £1,500/mo × 12 = **£72k recurring revenue**.
  - Total = **~£156.5k/year**.
- Boutique team model (4 consultants):
  - 4 × 130 billable days × £750/day = **£390k services revenue**.
  - Plus 10 retainers × £2,500/mo × 12 = **£300k recurring revenue**.
  - Total = **~£690k/year** before software licensing upside.
- Upside with productized software licensing:
  - Adds higher-margin recurring revenue and improves valuation multiple versus pure services.

Note: the scenarios are modeled estimates based on observed current rates/pricing; they are not a claim about any specific firm’s actual P&L.

## 5) Business Model Fit for Agent Ops

## 5.1 Recommended commercial structure

- Layer 1: Subscription license for software platform.
- Layer 2: On-site/implementation package (fixed scope).
- Layer 3: Ongoing managed retainer (optimization, monitoring, playbooks, governance).

## 5.2 Why this fits the data

- Buyers need operational trust and implementation support (barriers: cost, skills, security, reliability).
- Agentic project failure rates reward vendors who own rollout discipline.
- Hybrid model captures:
  - Initial integration budget (project cash flow).
  - Ongoing optimization budget (retainer MRR).
  - Scalable software margin (license ARR).

## 5.3 Packaging reference from Laravel ecosystem

- Laravel Nova shows viable per-user paid licensing.
- Laravel Spark shows viable paid subscription tooling model.
- Practical implication for your pricing architecture:
  - Per-workspace or per-operator license.
  - Higher tiers tied to governance/compliance features, multi-runner support, and SLA-backed support.

## 6) UK GTM Focus for Consultancy-Led Sales

- Priority verticals:
  - Financial services (high AI adoption, high compliance burden).
  - Professional/business services (workflow-heavy, repetitive knowledge tasks).
  - Mid-market operations teams with clear automation ROI (back office, support ops, internal tooling).
- Buyer roles:
  - COO, Head of Operations, CTO/Engineering Director, Transformation Lead.
- Best first offer:
  - 2-4 week “Agentic Workflow Discovery + Pilot” with explicit ROI and risk controls.
- Land-and-expand motion:
  - Pilot -> production workflow set -> monthly reliability/governance retainer -> broader license footprint.

## 7) Risks Investors Will Ask About (and Mitigations)

- Risk: Agentic project failure/cancellation rates.
  - Mitigation: stage-gated delivery, measurable ROI gates, observability and governance defaults.
- Risk: Public-sector consulting budget pressure.
  - Mitigation: private-sector-first wedge; product-led margin mix; strict value-based packaging.
- Risk: Services-heavy model caps multiple.
  - Mitigation: steadily shift revenue mix toward recurring software license + standardized managed services.
- Risk: Fast-moving incumbent platforms.
  - Mitigation: verticalized templates, UK compliance posture, faster implementation cycle times, local service moat.

## 8) Deck-Ready Narrative (Suggested Slide Flow)

- Problem: UK firms want AI automation, but delivery fails on governance, skills, and reliability.
- Market timing: UK AI adoption has crossed majority in target business sizes; consulting and AI budgets are active.
- Product + service wedge: Agent Ops + implementation + ongoing managed reliability.
- Revenue model: project cash + retainer MRR + software ARR.
- Proof of monetization: current day rates, current package pricing, enterprise AI booking evidence.
- Defensibility: operational know-how, reusable playbooks, governance rails, domain-specific rollout patterns.
- Expansion path: UK mid-market -> regulated sectors -> partner/consultancy channel.
