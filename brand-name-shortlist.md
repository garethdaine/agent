## Verification Notes

- **RDAP verified** = Checked against Verisign's authoritative .com WHOIS database (rdap.verisign.com). A 404 response means the domain is genuinely unregistered. This is the most reliable check possible without attempting to purchase.
- **⚠️ Unknown** on social handles means instantusername.com couldn't determine status (usually means available — the platform returned no public profile). You should verify directly by attempting to register the handle.
- **Buy domains immediately** if you pick one — these were available as of March 6, 2026 but could be registered at any time.
- All "agent*" prefix .com domains are essentially gone. The "ops*" prefix still has pockets of availability.

## My Recommendation

**OpsMarch** is the strongest candidate. It has a direct .com (opsmarch.com), the cleanest social handle availability (4 confirmed available out of 5 required, with Instagram likely available too), and the name instantly communicates "operations moving forward with purpose." **OpsGlide** is the runner-up if you prefer a softer, more "smooth/premium" feel over the assertive "march forward" energy.

If you're willing to use a get-prefix domain, **OpsRail** (getopsrail.com) has a perfect clean sweep on all 5 social platforms and "Rail" is a strong metaphor for infrastructure and guided operations.
